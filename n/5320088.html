Retired hero Chen Ai opened a small tavern at the frontier of the empire.

He thinks he would live a peaceful life for the rest of his days.

Unexpectedly, as the tavern opened, he encountered a formidable opponent in his life!

"You Dare to open a tavern opposite my tavern? How many months do you think your tavern can survive?"

Appearing before our hero is a stunningly beautiful elf girl and her invincible five-star hotel. A rookie in the service industry, faces the Demon King.

Facing The risk of closure just after opening the tavern?!

As a standing man, I swear by the name of the hero that I will make you my maid in the end! A lesbian elf, a former lover, a cold and elegant princess, and a beast-eared loli.

With more and more people gathering, ultimately a force that could change the world will be formed!
