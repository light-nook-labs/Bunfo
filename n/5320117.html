The Wars in the Blue Sky

简介

Colonel Carl Neinheim, the ace pilot, was killed in the Battle of Hurricane Fortress. However, instead of returning to the cycle of reincarnation, he woke up to find his soul trapped inside the remains of the sorcerer King Lielle Brynstadt. As a soldier, he was immediately thrown into chaos. Luckily, he received help from a wizard and embarked on a journey to discover his destiny.

Thus, the fantasy tale filled with love and justice, "The Wars in the Blue Sky," begins!
