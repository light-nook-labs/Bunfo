Magic

Machine & Magic & Another World

OtherWorld War SteamPunk Gardening

简介

An engineering genius crossed over to the magical world, but what exactly is magic? In Norfy's explanation, fire magic is about controlling highly-energized plasma, frost magic is forcibly stopping molecular thermal motion, earth magic involves manipulating molecular arrangement to form predetermined lattice growth, and as for wind magic, that's too simple, next!

What? There are gods in this world too? Even if someone is a god, they still need approval from the organization before taking office. Believing in materialism is what makes a qualified god!

Norfy, use science and industry to create a prosperous career in this magical world!
