简介

Preface:

1. It is a style that you have never seen before.

2. This text has a certain level of mental leaps, proceed with caution.

Synopsis:

In the Middle Ages, the hearts of witches hung on the cross with no emotions;

In a wasteland world, the pupils of the wounded slaves were dim and lifeless;

The imperial princess, who traveled among various asteroids to evade pursuit, was lonely and desperate.

...

Wu Xian thought he had awakened the Dream System,

As a responsible hot spring hotel owner, he took it upon himself to resolve the emotional entanglements of young girls.

Entering their nightmares and putting an end to nightmares became his daily task.

However, recently, he discovered that more and more neighbors had moved in nearby,

And every single one of them was a beautiful girl whose nightmares he had resolved.

This... doesn't seem right at all!

This book is also known as "Saving the Desperate Girls", "I'm Really Not the Mastermind Behind the Scenes".
