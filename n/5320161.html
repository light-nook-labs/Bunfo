Becoming a City-Toppling Beauty

Mecha Transsexual

简介

He died in the heat of vengeance—only for fate to grant him a second life… as a girl. And not just any girl, but the kind of beauty that turns heads and invites trouble.

Too afraid to face those from her past, she melts into the city’s neon haze, hiding her name and nursing her grudge in silence—only to find that quiet schemes don’t topple mountains.

But in this mortal maze, beauty can be a blade and charm a snare. Old debts awaken, new entanglements bloom, and the red strings knot into a garden of lilies.

A yuri harem tale of rebirth, revenge, and a face too stunning for peace.
