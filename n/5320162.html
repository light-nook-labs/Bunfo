It's Tough Being the Villainess's Big Sister

Fantasy OtherWorld Transsexual

简介

Adele was the picture of perfection—top of her class, gentle and gracious by nature. With snow-white hair and crimson eyes that even the most exacting devotees of beauty couldn’t fault, many said she was an angel sent to the House of Douglas, born to be a royal consort.

She believed it too, until the year she turned twelve and took her first magic aptitude test.

After that day, everything seemed to change. Father and Mother no longer pulled her into warm embraces, no longer indulged her tender whims. She didn’t know why, and she tried with all her might to win back their love in other ways.

But the moment she saw the black-haired girl step over their threshold, she understood that all her efforts were in vain.

“Adele, this is your new sister.”

“Her name is Mila.”

“From today on, treat her as family.”

In the way Father and Mother looked at that black-haired girl, Adele saw what used to be hers.

Five years later, when Adele shattered the girl’s shin and listened to that sweet cry of agony, she couldn’t help but think back to that moment.

She thought: that was when the seed of hatred first sprouted.
