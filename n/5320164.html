Elf Maiden in Another World

Fantasy Invincible Transsexual

简介

“I want to return to the past—to set out at the beginning and walk to the end of all things.”

The elf maiden lifts her gaze to the vault of heaven and whispers to the stars. She cuts through mountain-high, raging seas, endures white, frost-bitten skies, quenches a heaven-devouring blaze of wrath, and at last crosses a death-scented wasteland to reach the far shore of truth.

“It wasn’t that bad.” Faced with her beloved’s soft, worried voice upon her return, she brushes off an epic fit for legend as if it were nothing.

“Huh?” Yet when her beloved bares her true colors—with the sly decree of a main consort—fear slowly blooms across the elf’s face.
