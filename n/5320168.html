Maidens’ Nocturne

简介

In a wondrous world where technology and magic entwine...

A lone boy journeys to the New Continent, only to be swept—who knows why—into a chain of incidents...

And in the end... he can only live in the shape of a girl...

Worse yet, girls keep pinning her down day after day—utterly mortifying!

It begins with a hint of mystery, but its heart is girls’ love and adventure.

A warm, affectionate tale—cute without being brainless—brimming with imagination.
