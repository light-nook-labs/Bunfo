I Transmigrated into the Game as a Vampire

Fantasy Invincible LevelUp Transsexual

简介

He transmigrated, becoming his in-game avatar in a world of sword and sorcery—and was, by all rights, invincible.

Except the avatar he became was a Level 320, maxed-out vampire… a pint-sized loli. Naturally, that came with its own woes.

She decided to hide her fangs, masquerade as a young noble master, and coast through a quiet, eat-and-wait-to-die kind of life.

But this young master is trouble incarnate! Assassins lurk at every turn, bizarre beauties keep zeroing in on him, and at the worst possible moment he even gets kicked out of the family!

Shaking with cold fury, she groused: So this world won’t let a girl live a normal life, huh? Can a vampire not catch a break? Do I really have to unleash my ultimate secret art and blast the lot of you to kingdom come before you’re satisfied?
