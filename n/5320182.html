(Gender-Bender GL, 1v1)

Jet-propelled airships soared overhead, while colossal engines thundered across the earth. Without question, this was a world steeped in steam, where the advent of the difference engine had hurled technology into unprecedented bloom.

And me?

Reborn with my memories into a realm utterly unlike the last, I still lived by the book—the boldest risk I’ve taken this life was switching careers, trading a cushy “blessed” nine-to-five for a professorship in arcane studies.

What else has changed?

Heidi tipped her chin skyward, thinking: study forbidden black magic, break through the uncanny Black Realm, thrash scions of noble houses, get dragged off to prison as a witch, become the Empire’s center of attention... Hmph. Truly, a life as plain as water.
