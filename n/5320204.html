The So-Called Hero Sent to Slay the Demon King—Dies, Revives, and Wakes as a Girl... and the Demon King’s Slave?!

Dispatched by the emperor to vanquish the Demon King, the so-called hero watched his forces be wiped out until only he remained. In the end, he fell as well—felled by a single thrust of the Demon King’s blade.

Yet the Soul Crystal’s summons dragged him back into this world. However… why did the hero awaken as a girl? And why has she somehow become the Demon King’s slave?

This isn’t a voyage of smooth sailing—it’s a winding, heartwarming tale.
