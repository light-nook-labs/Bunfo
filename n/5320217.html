Outshining My Idol Little Sister in Feminine Charm? Give Me a Break

Love

简介

I have a sister I’m proud of, a father who—even in death—probably still spits on me from his grave, and a mother who remarried and brought home another set of sisters. But none of that mattered. By then, another woman had already warped me beyond repair. From that summer onward, my quiet life veered onto a road with no way back, and my blood sister, because of it, carved a singular, blazing arc across the night.

All right, maybe I’m being dramatic. I’m just a pervert. But have you heard that perversion is contagious? This is the tale of a whole brood of perverts.
