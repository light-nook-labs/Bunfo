简介

A shameless god conned me into a silver-haired, scarlet-eyed villainess—a Dragon Princess—and I even accidentally bound the heroine and stashed her in the basement. I, Su Qingqian, just wanted to clear my name.

Standing before me is a poised, heartbreakingly demure big-sister type with her cultivation sealed—she’s also the future protagonist’s fiancée.

Choose: go all-in and make the brainwashing one-and-done? Record a naughty little video for easy blackmail? Long live the scoundrel.

I had no choice before; now I only want to be a good person.

Yet the harder I try to steer the heroines off their corruption routes, the more dangerous their eyes become when they look at me.

Bad women, don’t you dare trick me—I bite. Back off! What, you plan to ride the dragon?
