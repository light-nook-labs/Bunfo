The Villainous Little Dragon Princess Refuses to Be Their Canary in a Gilded Cage

Invincible Transsexual

简介

After Pulling an All-Nighter and Dropping Dead, Rui’er Became an Evil Dragon—a Frail Villainess Dragonling Princess the Hero Is Supposed to Hack to Pieces

Luckily, Rui’er knows a thing or two about the plot. With a bit of effort, she should be able to dodge that tragic fate… right?

So Rui’er begins to move from the shadows.

And to restore her strength, she quietly kindles a few clandestine romances with other girls.

But she never expected this:

Why is the hero—with a possessiveness bar through the roof—on a second loop and already blackened?

Why is her pure-white little sword spirit actually a smiling blade, forever trying to strong-arm her into doing questionable things?

And the soft, sweet girl she mooched free meals from… turns out to be the final boss of the villain organization?

Staring at the circle of girls closing in on her with strange expressions, Rui’er feels her face drain of color.

It’s over. This is about to go very, very wrong.

[Redemption, Dominant S, 1vMany, Pseudo-Invincible]
