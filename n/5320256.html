So Many Vanquished Heroines, and I’m the Lone Evil Dragon

Love Comedy Transsexual

简介

Reborn as a dragonling princess among demonkind, her shy, inward nature left her ostracized—demoted to the lowest rung, a nobody tasked with guarding the cells.

Far from the treacherous intrigues of the demon court, all she had to do each day was boil water, deliver meals to captives, and drift through idle hours. Lilia found the quiet oddly blissful, even a touch delightful.

Months later, the demons suffered heavy losses in their war against the human kingdoms. Lilia, who thought it had nothing to do with her, was forcibly conscripted and hurled to the front line.

“You—go wipe out the Hero’s party!”

“Huh? Me, fight the Hero? You’re kidding, right?”

Ready to die, she grit her teeth and stepped onto the battlefield, mind racing for any way to survive—only to see the imperious lady Hero collapse at the hem of her little skirt.

“Guh… this dragon is just too cute—I mean too strong! As bitter as it feels, a loss is a loss. Take me prisoner and do with me as you please!”

“?”

From then on, noble princesses, holy nuns, and alluring elves fell one after another to Lilia, and things began to feel very, very off…
