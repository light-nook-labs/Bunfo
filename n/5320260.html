Run Over by a Dump Truck, Reborn with the Demon King’s Seven Sins System

After a dump truck put an end to Lin En’s first life, he was reborn in another world bound to a Demon King System—collect the Seven Deadly Sins to complete his awakening.

A master inventor, Lin En quickly found methods that suited him: smutty paperbacks to harvest Lust, NTR novels to stoke Wrath...

But before long, he realized he could seize Demon King Authorities with the simplest of tasks. Like giving his own silver-haired dragoness a massage in front of his saintess fiancée; downing a potion from a junior schoolmate while the two of them were alone; pinning his household’s blonde vampire “big sister” to the wall and teasing her.

…Are the system’s quests really just being handed to him on a silver platter?
