简介

After losing big on soccer bets in his past life, a young man is reborn in another world as an elven maiden—and awakens to the Thief class.

With no real skill to make fast money, she scrapes by on a meager wage at a bakery by day. To pad her purse, she can only resort to petty tricks and light fingers.

After luring an out-of-town adventurer into the mines and swindling him out of every coin, Kelsey is counting her spoils, smug and triumphant, when a knock sounds at the door.

“Coming—who is it?”

“It’s me,” comes the calm reply.

Oh no. That voice—the adventurer. Absolutely. Do. Not. Open. The door!!!
