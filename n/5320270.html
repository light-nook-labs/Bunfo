简介

“You guarded me for six years, loved me for so long. Only now do I understand what love truly is. For the rest of my life, it’s just you and me—I won’t let you go.”

“I’m your true childhood sweetheart. I was here first—there’s no way I’m going to lose.”

“I’m your future wife. Whatever it takes, I’m the only one who can stay by your side for a lifetime, okay?”

After five years of secret longing, Yun Mingxin’s year-long romance ends in heartbreak. Swearing off love, he braces for a quiet life—until the unexpected crashes in: a cool-as-ice heiress ex, an innocent twin-tailed childhood sweetheart, a campus belle who insists she’s his wife-to-be, and an elegant Japanese teacher who even arrives with her own maid. Why are they all suddenly chasing him?
