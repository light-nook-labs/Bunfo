After Abandoning My Daughters, Will They Still Call Me Father?

OtherWorld Cultivation Gxb Transsexual

简介

Reborn as the Strongest Hero, Dorothy Still Fell.

Captured, the unyielding Dorothy was enthralled by the Demon Queen and stripped of her memories.

She then fell in love with the Queen, became her consort, and bore a child.

For five honeyed years she lived at the Queen’s side; after yet another breathless confession, a shard of her past suddenly surfaced.

Damn.

She used to be a Hero.

That very night, she left her two-year-old daughter behind and fled the palace.

Entangled in love and hate, she hid her name and withdrew from the world.

Years later, she unexpectedly crossed paths with her grown daughter.

Only…

The girl who should have been a demon princess—why is she a Hero?

Haunted by guilt, Dorothy conceals her identity, guarding the child from the shadows while hunting for the truth.

—

“So you’re not only the strongest Hero—you’re our mother?”

Upon learning the truth, the daughters stared at the frail woman they’d tried so hard to protect and fell into silence.

Dorothy: …

Could someone please tell her… why does she have more than one daughter?!
