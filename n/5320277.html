简介

Yuri (GL) 1v1 Pure Romance—Reborn as a witch but booted from the hero’s party once the quest is over, Mei Yige is forced to toil as an otherworldly wage slave just to survive. To ease the rent, she decides to find a roommate.

In the end, she finds a blood demon.

“I don’t really have requirements for a roommate—just don’t interrupt my work.”

As landlord and housemate, the blood demon Miss Shi Ren is a total sweetheart. She only asks Mei Yige to chip in for upkeep and handle some chores; with that, her little quirk of snacking on humans becomes… surprisingly acceptable.

But…

“Sister Shi, could you put away your little snacks? My ice cream’s getting blood on it.”

“Sister Shi, don’t use the jar I keep my canned peaches in to store heads, hey!”

“Sister Shi, is that meat any good? Break me off a piece…”

The bewildered murderess is utterly baffled by her new roommate’s dense obliviousness—and that even stronger, unmistakably nonhuman aura.

Being called “Sister Shi” day after day makes Shi Ren give up the idea of turning Mei Yige into ingredients, and start considering another possibility: a partner.

Until one day, the hero and the other former teammates show up, hoping to reminisce about their old camaraderie with Miss Mei.

Mei Yige ties up her ex-party members and turns to Shi Ren, aggrieved:

“I didn’t throw a party—they came on their own. Sister Shi, if you eat them, you can’t eat me, okay?”

Realizing this little fox is both calculating and delightfully black-hearted, Shi Ren relaxes with a smile and cups her face.

“That won’t do, Miss Mei. I want it all.”

Contains: female-centric, voyeurism, revenge, abstract titles and mini-theater, obedience-style conditioning, PUA…
