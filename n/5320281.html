简介

After Sudden Death, I Woke in Another World—And Not Even a Reliable Cheat?

Yi Han didn’t think much of it. By day he basked in the sun; by night he chose a dream to wander. Leisure came easy.

Then he realized something was off. Why did dream and waking life blur so closely? Why were more and more people coming for him?

A witch who bent heat and flame to her will smiled at him. “I finally found you.”

A mute little mermaid stood before him, dagger in hand—refuse her, and she’d kidnap him on the spot.

Cinderella, now sovereign of a nation, opened a gilded cage. “Remember? This was our home.”

At last, Yi Han understood: every dream he’d walked through had once been reality.

And now, the girls with whom he shared those karmic entanglements have all come knocking.
