The Demonesses I Saved Keep Knocking at My Door

Fantasy OtherWorld Love

简介

Having completed every mission the System set and saved the maidens teetering on the brink of demonhood, Xia’er finally earned the quiet, peaceful life he’d longed for.

Yet they came all the same.

The empress sisters of an ancient dynasty seek to renew the thread of fate;

The cursed dragon maiden in her tower vows never to be parted again;

The clockwork goddess who commands the stars longs to be his companion for eternity;

...

Those girls haven’t truly changed. They remain—and will forever be—fiends.

But this time, what drives them is no longer the desire to conquer the world—it is the desire to conquer Xia’er himself.
