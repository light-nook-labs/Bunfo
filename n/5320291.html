“She might be a decent person. Maybe you two could just be friends?”

“Babe, I think the campus belle’s a creep—I swear she followed me the whole way.”

“Maybe… you just happened to be heading the same direction?”

“Babe, the campus belle just confessed to me. What do I do?”

“If you truly like her, I don’t mind.”

“That’s what only scumbags would do. Not me—I only love my babe.”

The campus belle stared at the messages on her screen, dazed. Damn this ironclad straight man—he still hasn’t recognized me!
