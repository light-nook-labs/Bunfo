Today, I Want to Quit the Party—Again

Fantasy OtherWorld DemonKing&Brave Adventure

简介

Another day of watching the Hero and his girlfriends being all lovey-dovey…

Jettley eyed the three on the far end of the carriage, laughing and chatting, and thought so.

Another day of wanting to quit the party…

He stared at the wooden planks beneath his boots and thought so.

Tell me—just a few months ago we watched the Hero cut down the Demon King with his own blade. How did that bastard pop back up?

I was this close to becoming one of those quit-the-party protagonists. Where did it all go wrong?

A prickle of suspicion stirred in Jettley’s chest.

He studied the Hero’s profile—so breathtakingly pretty it put his orientation in serious jeopardy—and fell into a daze.

He didn’t notice the faint blush creeping up the Lord Hero’s crystalline earlobe.

Was it guilt? Or shy embarrassment?

Perhaps… a bit of both?
