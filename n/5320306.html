What’s So Wrong with Being a Son-in-Law?

As a live-in son-in-law, I get to eat her family’s rice—soft rice is easy on the teeth.

So what if she’s a Dragon Princess? She can’t sleep unless I climb into her bed.

Transmigrated as a noble in another world, Lan Che wants nothing more than to read in the sun, stroll the streets, and live a carefree, lazy life.

Except the woman he’s becoming a live-in husband for turns out to be a Dragon Princess?!

Worse, the Kingdom Princess, the Empire Princess, the Demon Princess, the Elf Princess, and even a Princess of the Divine Realm all keep barging in, wrecking his slacker paradise!

“Don’t you want to have a child with me?” asks the Dragon Princess.

“Don’t you want to beat my war drum?” asks the Kingdom Princess.

“I come with a dowry,” says the Empire Princess.

“Put your pants on and you think you can slack off?” the Demon Princess snarls.

“Obey me, and I’ll reward you~” the Divine Princess purrs.

Damn these enchantresses—they’re shattering my Dao heart!
