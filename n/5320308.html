How Could a Wicked Woman Make the Ice-Cold Persona Crumble

Love Daily Gxb

简介

“So you’re saying you’ve got amnesia now, so you can’t remember any of that?”

“Yes.”

The boy guiltily shifted his gaze away.

“Then… that landlady who teaches—the one who keeps squatting by your door—she’s worried about you?”

“…What teacher?”

“And that policewoman who keeps talking about protecting you, but in reality tails and stalks you—she’s doing it for your own good too?”

“…Huh?”

“And all those other bizarre women, sigh…”

Seeing the boy’s utterly blank expression, the girl sighed and took his hand.

“You still have a mission. Come back with me.”

“I’m not going back to that little dark room!”

As if a trigger word had been hit, he panicked and flung her hand away.

“Oh?”

Her eyes narrowed, dangerous as a blade; at the same time, those inexplicably blackened women caught up and boxed him into a corner.

“Didn’t you say you had amnesia?”

“Didn’t you say it was only me?”

“Didn’t you say you’d let me protect you for life?”

“Didn’t you say…”

What is wrong with these women?! Weren’t they supposed to help?

Crap! I’m so screwed!
