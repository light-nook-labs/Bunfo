简介

As a socially anxious, internet-addicted shut-in, Hua Xin’s greatest joy was chugging a four-yuan jumbo iced black tea while gaming all day—then, come night, indulging in some soft, squishy gel.

But on this day, after hitting “one day first four over” and crashing out, he woke to find that Moo-Moo, unable to tolerate his mistreatment, had run away from home.

Hua Xin becomes a raven-haired beauty with sleek, straight locks—but the new semester is fast approaching.

When Asperger’s-driven social fear, Moo-Moo’s disappearance, the discomforts of a sudden transformation, and boundless anxiety all strike at once…

Hua Xin chooses to lie flat.

Yet when school can no longer be avoided, a promise forces her to face the world outside.

Meanwhile, her class monitor Xia Yan—who shares the same history of autism and solitude—vows to help his “roommate” and “brother” step out of the shadows, grow bright and independent, and stand tall against the world.

Until one day, Hua Xin discovers that the “Shrimp-Head” guy she’d been eager to e-date and farm gold with… is actually the very same Xia Yan who’s been caring for her???!!

Who would have thought the gentle, sunlit, ever-considerate class monitor is a lewd, mischievous gremlin online??

Once the mask slips, won’t the only ending be turning into a cream puff…?
