OtherWorld DemonKing&Brave

简介

An invincible tale with a slice-of-life bent.

Cast into another world, the protagonist climbs to Level 999—a supreme powerhouse. Yet he has no taste for crowns or conquest; he yearns only for days of wine and song. Now and then he’ll put on airs, playing the villainous Demon King; sometimes he’ll play mentor to a few young ladies. He steals lovers and hearts alike, and lives his days free and easy.
