简介

Chu Nan has lived twice—both transmigrated and reborn—and these days he’s just a small-time streamer with a hundred thousand fans.

He figured coasting through this life wouldn’t be so bad, until a sudden twist shattered his peace.

Out of nowhere, a system binds to him, promising to grant his heart’s desires—and even drops a breathtaking beauty onto his bed. Since when do systems ship with a bride?

Before he can celebrate, the system drops another bomb: this “wife” is Xiao Han, his childhood friend—the one he was so close to they practically shared a pair of shorts.

Faced with a maiden of a hundred charms, Chu Nan wrestles with himself for a long, painful beat… then finally asks his first question: “Wanna do it?”
