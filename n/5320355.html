I Became the Ice-Queen Wife, But I Don’t Want to Be Hated

Love Comedy Transsexual

简介

Jiang Lai was an ordinary single father. When his daughter turned one, his childhood-sweetheart wife left this world.

For over a decade, he raised his girl alone—until one morning he woke up a woman, and not just any woman: a married lady with her allure still in full bloom.

First, his beloved daughter mistook her for a homewrecker and beat her black and blue; now, trapped in a woman’s body, she endures her daughter’s scorn day in and day out.

An icy, high-powered female boss dangled work over her head, pushing casting-couch rules and wanting Jiang Lai tucked under her wing—and in her bed.

After losing her job, she turned to streaming, only for the number-one rich patroness on her leaderboard to demand “benefit” photos and insist on an offline meeting.

The sister-in-law who had always been gentle locked her in a room, whispering taboo words against her ear from dawn to dusk.

And the only kind, gentle little white flower at her side turned out to be her daughter’s college roommate. Is there anyone normal left around her?
