Magic

When an Unparalleled Beautiful Youth Enters a World Ruled by Women

Love Invincible

简介

In One Thought, a Gentleman; in the Next, an Asura...

In ages past, peerless in grace yet crushed beneath a weight too great to bear, sleepless through countless restless nights—

Now, with every memory lost, the burden cast off, he drifts through the mortal world like duckweed upon the tide.

Of old, emperors waged war across the realm for the smile of a beauty.

But now, women of exalted power vie against one another for the fleeting smile of a young man!

A beauty may bring calamity to a kingdom;

a handsome face may bring ruin to a nation...
