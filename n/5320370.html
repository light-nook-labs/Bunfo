I, the Strongest Saint, Don’t Want to Fall in Love with the Saintess

Love Invincible Comedy

简介

There are twelve Saints in this world, each wielding unparalleled power and revered by all beneath the heavens. Among them, the strongest was Maurice, whose other awe-inspiring title was that of the youngest Holy Pope in the Duchy of Luna. And yet, even a man like him had something he could never defy—

“Get lost! To hell with the Holy Maiden of the Sacred Church! I refuse an arranged marriage! The princess wants to be betrothed to me too? I’d rather marry a prince! My childhood sweetheart? No, no, no—that’s my sister!”

And so, one day, driven to despair when his own teacher personally forced a marriage upon him… he chose to run.
