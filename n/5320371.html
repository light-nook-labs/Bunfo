Can the Heroine Captured After Defeat Still Find Happiness?

简介

Defeat CG for the Hero? But the Hero’s a Guy!

Then just turn him into a girl.

And so, after the strongest Hero from another world met the delightfully inevitable fate of being defeated by the evil dragon...

He became a delicate, captivating silver-haired dragon girl, taken captive by the Dragon Queen, while losing all memory of ever being the Hero.

Many years later, the Hero-sama—now a dragon girl—spent her days as a gentle, devoted mother, caring for the children, and her nights attending upon the exalted Queen of Dragonkind.

Then, by a twist of fate...

The Heroine regained her memories.

Faced with her questioning, the Dragon Queen answered:

“Yes. You were once the Hero. And now, we have two children—one already born, and one yet unborn.”

“No!!!”

Clutching her belly, the Heroine burst into tears.

So... does a Hero who became the Dragon Queen’s consort after losing in battle still have the right to find happiness?
