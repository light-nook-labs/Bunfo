A Slow-Burn Campus Romance, Clean and Drama-Free

“Mr. Chen, rumor has it that after your rebirth, you devoted yourself to studying hard and building a business from the ground up, never once bothering with romance—and that’s how you achieved your success today. Is that true?”

“Of course. Dating? Even dogs wouldn’t bother!”

“Then may I ask—there are also rumors that you have a very, very close friend, so close that the two of you once hoped to bear the fruit of your friendship?”

“Impossible. We’re innocent—utterly innocent!”

“Very well. Then what about the rumor that you have a wealthy white moonlight willing to lower her score just to attend the same university as you?”

“That’s impossible too. I just owe her money—I’ve got no intention of selling myself!”

“Then is it true that you’re being wrapped around the finger of a certain bad woman?”

“Th-That… hss... how about we just go with the first two rumors instead?”
