Miss Spy, You Wouldn’t Want the Mission to Fail, Would You?

OtherWorld Transsexual

简介

After turning into a girl, Loco wakes up inside a novel she once read. On the surface, she’s the heroine’s trusted teammate. In truth, she’s a spy planted at the heroine’s side—one destined to betray her without mercy.

To avoid repeating that disastrous fate, Loco decides she’s done being a spy… except she really has no choice.

With her life held in the hands of the villainous young lady, she can only work for her in secret while somehow keeping up appearances with the heroine.

But… how, how did things end up like this?!

“I actually knew from the start that you were a mole. But why have you never made a move against me? Don’t tell me… you have feelings for me, so you couldn’t bring yourself to do it?”

“I told you to get close to her, not to get this close!”

Faced with the heroine’s questioning and the villainess’s furious demand for answers, Loco freezes.

Since when did she somehow max out both of their affection meters?

So now… whose side is she supposed to choose?

Somebody save her—she’s just a spy with survival instincts cranked to the limit!
