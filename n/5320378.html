Who Hid the Secret in Hell?

Adventure Inference Horror

简介

Strange tombs lie strewn across the seas and mountains, uncanny graves adorning the rivers and the land.

Throughout the Divine Realm of Huaxia, rare and jagged places abound, drawing wronged bones to rest beneath towering peaks.

How much do you truly know of this world’s secrets?

A thousand-year-old underground palace lies sealed beneath the lakebed, while undying shades wander the paths between tombs.

The eerie ancient corpse standing in the dark—who is it smiling at?

The decayed figure seated beside the old coffin—who is it whispering to?

Wei Shenji, an ordinary high school girl, is swept overnight into a string of bizarre events.

Was it mere chance, or destiny long ordained?

Forced to venture into baleful tombs, Wei Shenji survives by the narrowest margin, escaping death again and again.

Are vengeful spirits truly coming to claim lives, or is it all an elaborate deception?

To grasp the meaning hidden within the game, one must kiss the Spider Lily of the Other Shore.
