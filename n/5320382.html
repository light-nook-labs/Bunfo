Good news: Noah transmigrated into a corpse—and was even dug up from a grave, only to be brought back to life.

Bad news: they dug up the wrong grave, and now they’re seriously considering stuffing him back in!

Fortunately, they offered compensation and made Noah their guild master.

But Noah soon realized that something about all this was deeply, terribly off...

A ritual mage with a one hundred percent failure rate.

A dimwitted, big-eating birdfolk warrior.

A nun-priestess whose only skill is blowing herself up.

Even the gentle elven maid turns out to be a certified lunatic with ten years of experience.

Damn it! Why are all my teammates such useless disasters?!

I can’t take this anymore!

Fine—watch me whip them into shape!
