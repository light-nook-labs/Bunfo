简介

Otherworldly. Genderbender. Yuri.

Good news: I got reincarnated into another world!

Bad news: it’s one of those kicked-out-of-the-party novels.

Worse news: I’m the villainess noble lady captain—the one who does the kicking.

Even worse news: sooner or later, I’m going to get brutally obliterated by the revenge-driven protagonist and my absurdly overpowered former teammates.

So, to keep my life from being ruined by that superstition-obsessed former invincible heroine, the former haughty war princess whose love is far too heavy, the former yandere assassin drunk on blood, the scheming former demon spy, the former saintess whose faith has utterly collapsed... and a whole parade of other walking disasters—

I’ve decided to get close to them. Very, very close.
