The Bard Wishes to Sing an Epic of False Heroes

Fantasy Love Marriage Transsexual

简介

She was once the daughter of wealth—her parents gone, a fortune in the tens of millions left in her hands. So she set out to wander the world in search of adventure, only to fall and be reincarnated in another realm as a bard.

He had once been a member of a hero-class party, a team believed powerful enough to defeat the Demon King. Yet amid slander and relentless scorn, he was cast out, stripped of his adventurer’s status, and left to live in utter poverty.

“I wanted to sing epics of all kinds—so why is every single one of them about you?!” Caro finally snapped, shouting at the man.

The man spread his hands. “Sorry. I’m a false hero, after all. I’ve worn many names.”

This is the tale of a bard reborn as a beautiful girl and a false hero, as they journey across the world and leave behind legends of their own.
