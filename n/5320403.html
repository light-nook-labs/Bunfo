Reborn as a Demon Dragon Named Fafnir

A shut-in dragon has no interest in raiding towns—mostly because she has social anxiety.

She’s never even considered kidnapping a princess, either, because now she’s a girl herself.

Luckily, this world has the internet, so life isn’t all that dull. At most, she just gets a little snarky with people online.

That is, until she mouthed off to a dragon rider on the internet… and he came knocking at her door.

“Um... I was just joking. You believe me, right?”

Staring at the man before her, Fafnir—already hopelessly socially anxious—shrank back even further.

“Oh? Weren’t you running your mouth pretty boldly online? Go on, keep that same energy.”

Faced with the dragon rider’s relentless pressure, Fafnir didn’t even dare meet his eyes. She could only scream inwardly:

What are you so arrogant for, you dragon rider who doesn’t even have a dragon mount?!
