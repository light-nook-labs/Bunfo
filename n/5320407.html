简介

Not a Game, but the Tale of Two Worlds Intertwined

This is no game, but the story of two worlds merging into one: the real world, and a realm of magic.

O Tao, the world champion, gazed at his younger sister, frail and withering like a dead branch before his eyes. “Little Sis! No matter the cost, I’ll save you.”

“My lord father, you’re finally awake!” At the sight of the silver-haired girl by his bedside, he realized this was not a game, but another world. She was alive, warm, and real.

“You’ve been to the other world too, haven’t you?” the mixed-blood campus belle said with a smile as she picked up the game helmet and put it on.

“Mister, in the name of the princess, I challenge you to a knight’s duel!” The golden-haired princess lowered her longsword, magic flickering along its blade.

“Little Brother, I’m with you!” The domineering business queen wrapped her arms around him from behind.

“Hehe, I’m going to drain you dry~” The vampire princess pounced on him with a wicked grin.

Standing atop a hillside, O Tao turned his back on the glittering metropolis of neon and indulgence, and faced the magical continent reflected at the edge of the heavens.

“The game begins.”
