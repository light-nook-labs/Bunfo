A Villainess’s Life: From the Wheelchair-Bound Witch to the Perfect Housewife

PureLove Gxb Transsexual

简介

In a world steeped in eerie urban legends and unspeakable horrors, Jiang Qianyu felt that this life of his was nothing short of absurdly dramatic.

He adopted a daughter, only to find out she was a witch boss reborn into her childhood.

He obtained a wish-granting notebook—whatever was written in it would come true, so long as he paid the required price.

Yet under the deathly threat of his enemies, and in order to protect his daughter, the very first wish he made was to let her start life over once more.

Just when he thought his own life was about to come to a complete end—

through a twist of fate, the notebook arranged for him to start over as well, and he ended up becoming nominally mother and daughter with the daughter from his previous life.

The good news: she became a witch again.

The bad news: this witch is kind of weak.

Have you ever seen a witch who spends every day in a wheelchair?

“Who says a wheelchair-bound witch can’t count as an SSS-rank urban legend?!”

Just as Jiang Qianyu, full of confidence, set out to rewrite the future with sheer determination, he suddenly discovered that in this life... she seemed to be married.

And her spouse was a beautiful young heiress.

And this rich young woman, who had married a single mother just to dodge her family’s relentless pressure to wed...

was actually a Hunter of Horrors!

“Good grief, this world’s gone completely mad—at this rate, even mice are about to marry cats!”
