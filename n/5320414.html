“Pray tell… besides eating, sleeping, and slacking off, what else are you actually good at?”

The petite, cherry-haired loli puffed out her chest with boundless pride.

After being sent to the afterlife by a dog that fell from the sky, the boy began a brand-new life in another world—this time in the body of a loli.

As the leader of a colossal villain faction, she was determined to carve her way through the world with a personality forged at the “Half-Step Orange Cat Realm,” the “Great Perfection of the Sloth Realm,” and the “Peak of the Koala Realm”!

And yet, as she journeyed across the world, why was she becoming more and more popular?

She was the villain princess whose name struck fear across the land, not some pampered darling everyone wanted to protect!
