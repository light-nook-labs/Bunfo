Intermittent Body Swaps Between the Male and Female Leads

A Sweet 1v1 Pure Romance

Ever since Lu Yu started swapping bodies with the school beauty, he hadn’t had a single moment of peace.

He picked up the studies he had long abandoned, and even began learning the qin, chess, calligraphy, and painting...

In all his life, Lu Yu had never imagined he could work this hard.

Yet every time he got his own body back, he would be so drained he had to brace himself against the wall with both hands.

“What exactly did you do with my body?!”

The school beauty: “…If I said I caught a cold, would you believe me?”

—

All her glamour and grace were born of a gentle disguise.

The boy put on her mask, and pulled her out of the mire.

This is a story of saving, and being saved.
