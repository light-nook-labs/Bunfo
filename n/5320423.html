Compared to the crushing burden of saving the world, the villainess noble lady Amelia was a cold, breathtaking beauty with silver hair and violet eyes. Ifia only had to win over this rich lady to coast through life in comfort forever.

Suppressing the urge to drag the system down with her—

There’s still time. She has to run!!!
