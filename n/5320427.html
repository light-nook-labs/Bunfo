The Villainous Young Master’s Miserable Fate Must Be Because He Isn’t Evil Enough!

After learning that he had reincarnated as the villainous young master in a yuri otome game,

Morel resolved to track down the game’s heroine and kill her before the story could even begin.

And if anyone else got in his way, he’d take them out too.

But why was it that every time he took action with perfect enthusiasm, he was the one who ended up suffering?

He only meant to teach his pollen-allergic archenemy a lesson, yet somehow stumbled upon her darkest secret instead.

“How pitiful, Morel. Being tied up like that must be so uncomfortable, hm?”

The duke’s sadistic daughter smiled as she extended the tentacles hidden beneath her skirt.

And that stupid dog too—I may have accidentally fed you a magic potion,

but I didn’t do anything else!

Wait—skipping the fairy teacher’s Spirit Tongue class means getting dragged off for remedial lessons?!

No! Let me out!
