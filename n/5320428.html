The Shattered Realm

Battle Doomsday Adventure Inference

简介

Ming Yi, an utterly ordinary college student not even worthy of being called a backup, was assassinated by a mysterious shadow. When he opened his eyes again, the world he once knew had become a complete stranger.

Cities had crumbled into ruins. Aberrant beasts roamed unchecked. The fate of his companions remained unknown.

This was a world gone to ruin.

A world after death.

In a world without rules, if you were to meet someone truly precious, would you dare stand against the whole world for her?

How can these lost boys and girls once more go on to write an epic that belongs to the nameless?

If fate granted you one more chance to choose, would you leave this world a path to survive?
