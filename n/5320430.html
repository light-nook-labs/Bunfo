Also Known As: I Transmigrated Into the Villain, and Every Beautiful Girl Ended Up Under My Hypnosis! Keywords: Blond Punk, Hypnosis, Villain, Beautiful Girls...

When he opened his eyes, Gu Qinghan found himself transported into a bizarre game world built around hypnosis.

“I hereby swear, as a staunch believer in true love, I have never done anything unbecoming of a blond punk.”

“Hypnosis magic, luring girls, all that has nothing to do with me.”

“I’m truly just a law-abiding, upright citizen.”

In a sunless prison, Gu Qinghan sat in inmate garb, shackled hand and foot, answering with a straight face.

“Then may we ask—those girls standing outside, with vacant eyes and foolishly besotted smiles... who exactly hypnotized them?”

“That was the game’s villain. What does any of that have to do with me, Gu Qinghan?”
