After Learning My Wife Wasn’t Human, All I Wanted Was to Flee the Marriage

System Comedy Gxb

简介

After accidentally digging up an antique, he let greed get the better of him. Instead of turning it in, he brought it home.

From that very night on, he began dreaming—every single night—of a woman in red crawling into his room and pressing herself on top of him.

If it were you, what would you choose?

A. Bite your tongue and use the pain to force yourself awake.

B. Curse out her entire family.

C. Kiss her instead and make her blush.

That might work against one revenant—but what if there were two? Three? Could you still handle it?

As for our protagonist, his answer is simple:

He wants them all.

(This story is purely fictional. Please believe in science.)
