After I Kept My Distance, the Heroines Descended into Yandere Madness

Fantasy OtherWorld Love Gxb

简介

After Becoming the Distasteful Male Character in a Dark R16 Heroine Game—the Only One Capable of Replenishing the Heroines’ Mana—Hall Felt His Life Was Hanging by a Thread.

Because his entire purpose in the game was to serve as a mana battery whenever the chosen heroines were outmatched, helping them power up and level beyond their limits.

And in every route, he was ultimately doomed to die.

To save himself, he resolved to keep his distance from all the heroines.

Yet before long, the Demon God’s forsaken heir, the Church’s holy maiden, the Empress of the North, and the elven princess all came to him one after another, utterly ignoring the game’s original setting, and confessed their love.

But Hall knew the truth.

Little by little, they would all turn into dangerously obsessive yanderes—and when that happened, their threat level would skyrocket.
