He was the knight by the First Prince’s side.

She was the assassin sent by the Second Prince.

Raised from childhood to obey without question, she had never once defied her organization’s orders—even when they turned her into a woman and sent her to seduce the captain of the knights, she accepted it without hesitation.

Disguised as a little maid, she infiltrated the home of that knight who seemed so carefree and easygoing.

“He must never learn who I really am,” she said.

“Best not to alert the enemy yet. I’ll play along for now,” he said.

“To keep the other side steady, we should build some goodwill first.” — The two of them spoke in perfect unison.
