After Breaking Up, I Got Tangled Up with My Ex-Girlfriend’s Mother

In the wedding hall, Zhou You stepped forward to meet the beautiful bride—and the bridesmaid beside her, her face full of unwillingness.

“Thanks for the trouble.”

He thanked the bridesmaid, who had once been his girlfriend, and took her mother from her hands.

—

Zhou You had never imagined that after breaking up with his ex, his life would take such a turn.

The aloof, domineering older-woman CEO leaned close and whispered in his ear, “Do you want Big Sis to be your little kitten—and yours alone?”

The clingy heiress with an onee-san charm covered the entire city in flowers just to pursue him. “Big Sis likes you. Give me a chance.”

Enchanting, icy, clingy—one alluring older beauty after another appeared before him.

Other than the fact that his body could barely keep up, everything was great.

The female leads have no prior romantic experience.
